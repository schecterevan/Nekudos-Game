# Parent setup guide

**Opening the parent area:** press and hold the small lock button in the bottom corner for 2 seconds.

The **Setup** tab has a live checklist. Here are the same steps in more detail.

## 1. Match the teacher's pronunciation (Vowels & letters tab)

The app doesn't assume any pronunciation. Pick the closest starting point:

- **Modern Israeli style:** kamatz = patach, tzere = segol, tav sounds like tet.
- **Ashkenazi style:** kamatz "aw" ≠ patach "ah", tzere "ay" ≠ segol "eh", tav without dagesh sounds like "s".
- **Every symbol separate:** a blank slate.

Then go through the vowel and letter tables. **Sound group** is a label: symbols with the same label count as the same sound. Edit any row to match how her teacher reads. When it matches, tick **"I have checked the sound groups…"**. Until you do, "find by sound", letters, and reading stay locked, so she is never marked wrong for an answer that sounds right.

You can also rename any vowel or letter. The **Sound hint** column is just a reminder for you when recording.

## 2. Choose what to practice

- **Vowels:** tick one, several, or all. With **one vowel**, the game introduces it and gives single-card tapping practice. It never adds unselected vowels as distractors. Telling vowels apart needs at least two vowels, and "find by sound" needs two *different* sound groups.
- **Letters:** each written form is separate: בּ and ב, שׁ and שׂ, תּ and ת, and final forms. Only ticked letters ever appear.
- **Reading items:** every item shows exactly which letters and vowels it needs and whether it's in use. "Practice sequences" are syllables to sound out, not words. "Real words" show their meaning. Hide anything you don't want, and please check spellings with the teacher.

## 3. Record (Recordings tab)

Record in a quiet room, once, clearly, the way the teacher says it. You can record, upload a file, play, re-record, or delete. Silence is trimmed and the volume is evened out automatically. Missing recordings that the current activity needs are marked in red.

- **Vowel names:** "Kamatz".
- **Vowel sounds:** only the sound, never the name.
- **Letter + vowel:** the syllable, e.g. "ba".
- **Reading items:** the **whole** item as one clip.
- **Instructions & praise (optional):** "Tap the…", "Let's listen again", "Great job!". If you don't record these, the device voice can say them, or they're skipped.

The device voice is **never** used for vowel names, sounds, syllables, or words. The only exception is **Demo mode** (Activity tab), which shows a red "DEMO AUDIO" banner and is for previewing only.

If something is missing, the game leaves it out. If nothing is playable, she sees a "grown-up needed" picture instead of a broken question.

## 4. Pick the activity (Activity tab)

| Activity | What she does |
|---|---|
| Explore | Taps vowel cards to hear the name, then the sound |
| Find the vowel by name | Hears "Tap the kamatz", taps it |
| Find the vowel by sound | Hears only the sound, taps a matching vowel (same-sound vowels are all accepted) |
| Letters with vowels | Hears a syllable, taps it. Focus options: one letter with different vowels, or different letters with one vowel |
| Reading | Hears an item and taps it, **or** "Try reading, then tap to hear" (no judging, she just checks herself) |

You can also set the number of choices (2–4), questions per round (5 by default), max reading length, when to show the answer, reward sounds, background music, and the font.

## Princess dress-up

Each finished round gives her a present to tap open. Inside is the next princess item for her smiley: tiara, scepter, dress, hair, necklace, earrings, glass slippers, cape, rosy cheeks, castle, a unicorn to ride, then a rainbow, a flower crown for the unicorn, butterflies, a kitten friend, a giraffe friend, and fireworks to finish (17 in all). Every round ends in a present, because there's no losing. On the Activity tab you can turn this off or give a present every 2–5 rounds. The Progress tab shows how far she is and has a "Start dress-up over" button. To preview every step, open `dressup.html`.

## 5. Back up (Backup tab)

Settings, progress, and recordings live **only in this browser**. Clearing browser data, private browsing, or Safari's cleanup of unused sites can erase them. After recording, **Export a backup**, and keep the file safe. Import it on another device to move everything there. You can also tap "Ask the browser to keep this data".

## Progress tab

This shows, for each activity and each vowel, how often she was right the first time, right on a later try, or right after being shown. Items she needed help with come back more often, mixed with ones she knows. This is practice progress in this game, **not** a reading assessment.
